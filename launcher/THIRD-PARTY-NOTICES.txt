Race One Hub - Designed by LoZazaMastro

Authored builder UI and compatibility adapters
-------------------------------------------
The WPF theme and layout are adapted from The Simpsons Game PS5 Builder,
by LoZazaMastro and contributors, supplied by its author under GPL-3.0.
Changes include the orange/white/black theme, FM5 builder workflow, typed
backend protocol, input/output selection and authored numeric/tyre icons.
The GPL-3.0 license and corresponding project source are available at:
https://github.com/LoZazaMastro/RaceOneHub
No font file is included.
